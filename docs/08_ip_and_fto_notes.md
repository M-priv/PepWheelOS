# IP and Freedom-to-Operate Notes

This document is not legal advice. It is a scaffold for tracking IP and FTO questions.

## What to capture

For each campaign:

- Known peptide binders
- Known patents or patent families
- Published sequences
- Known target-binding motifs
- Assay methods that may be protected
- Manufacturing or formulation claims
- Novel modifications or scaffold features
- Trade-secretable internal data

## Possible defensibility layers

- Proprietary negative data
- Manufacturability failure data
- Target-specific design heuristics
- Validated peptide assets
- Agentic research graph
- Structured design-make-test-learn workflow
- Assay and synthesis outcome memory
- Internal scoring models
- Patentable sequences, uses, formulations or methods if applicable

## FTO checklist

- Is the exact sequence published?
- Are close analogues patented?
- Is the target already saturated with peptide claims?
- Are modifications, cyclisation strategies or use claims protected?
- Is the assay or manufacturing method restricted?
- Is there a clean research-use route?
