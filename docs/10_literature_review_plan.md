# Literature Review Plan

1. Peptide therapeutics and modality landscape
2. Cyclic peptide design
3. Protein language models
4. Structure prediction and peptide-target complexes
5. Molecular dynamics for peptide binding and stability
6. Peptide manufacturability and CMC
7. Toxicity, immunogenicity and developability prediction
8. Active learning and design-make-test-learn loops
9. AI agents in scientific research
10. Regulatory and quality expectations for peptide products

## Reading output format

For every paper, create a structured note:

- Citation
- Problem addressed
- Method
- Dataset
- Main result
- Limitations
- Relevance to peptide flywheel
- What system module it informs
- What should be implemented or avoided
