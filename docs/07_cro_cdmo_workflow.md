# CRO/CDMO Workflow

## Purpose

Convert peptide candidates into outsourced validation packages.

## Why this matters

An early AI peptide company should usually avoid building GMP manufacturing infrastructure. It should outsource synthesis and assays, then capture every returned result as structured data.

## CRO synthesis request package

Minimum fields:

- Candidate ID
- Sequence
- Modifications
- Cyclisation or stapling details
- Desired scale
- Desired purity
- Salt form
- Analytical confirmation requested
- Solubility notes
- Storage notes
- Known risk flags
- Intended assay use
- Questions for vendor

## CDMO/manufacturing intelligence

Capture:

- Quote
- Lead time
- Stated difficulty
- Crude purity
- Final purity
- Yield
- Analytical issues
- Vendor comments
- Failure modes
- Reorder decision

## Flywheel value

Vendor feedback becomes manufacturability training data.
